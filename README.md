# 📊 E-commerce Perfume Sales Analysis

This project performs an exploratory data analysis of men’s and women’s perfume sales in an e-commerce environment.

The goal is to identify sales patterns, product and brand performance, price vs sales relationship, and insights related to geography and inventory.

---

## 🔎 Key Insights

- Sales are concentrated in a small number of products and brands (long-tail effect).
- Mid-range priced products tend to perform better.
- Sales are not driven only by price, but also by brand strength and product availability.
- Certain regions concentrate most of the sales volume.
- There are signs of inventory imbalance (overstock and understock of high-demand items).

---

## 📁 Dataset

The dataset includes information such as:
- Product name and brand
- Price
- Units sold
- Location (city/state/country)
- Availability
